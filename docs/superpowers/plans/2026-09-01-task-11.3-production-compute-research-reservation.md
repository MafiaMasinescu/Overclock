# Task 11.3 Production Compute Research Reservation Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Execute this plan inline in the existing workspace. Do not commit or push; preserve all reviewed Task 11.1 and Task 11.2 changes.

**Goal:** Integrate the approved global Research reservation into the existing production Compute cache and witness path while preserving historical results, atomic Task delivery ownership, deterministic rollback, and the Task 11 boundary.

**Architecture:** Extend the existing per-`SimCore` Compute runtime cache with one private Research input projection; do not create a Research cache. Production Compute derives Research from facility available Compute after Power, Thermal, and Stability, passes the resulting factor to every Task calculation, and emits Research separately from the Task useful-delivery total. Fresh validation receives detached Research projection/result evidence, while stored historical Compute remains structural-only.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict mode, Vitest, existing `SimCore` structural-sharing tick systems, immutable JSON-compatible authoritative state, existing Compute topology/cache and exact witness APIs.

**Spec:** `docs/decisions/ADR-0017_DETERMINISTIC_RESEARCH_LIFECYCLE_AND_COMPUTE_RESERVATION.md` plus the approved Task 11.3 request.

## Global Constraints

- Extend the existing Compute cache; do not create an independent Research cache.
- Compute order is facility available Compute after Power/Thermal/Stability, Research result, Research factor, effective Task share, then Task Compute/bandwidth.
- Memory capacity remains unscaled; Research delivery is excluded from `totalAllocatedUsefulComputeFlops`.
- With no active Research, projection/result are `null`, `researchFactor` is exactly `1`, and Task 9 results remain unchanged.
- With reservation `R = 1`, active Tasks remain runnable with unchanged `requestedShare`, zero effective share/delivery, and full Research delivery.
- Only fresh calculation validation may compare the current Research branch; stored historical Compute is never reinterpreted against a later Research state.
- Progress-only changes are cache-compatible; node/share/null transitions invalidate Research and Task deliveries atomically.
- Later Task/Research stages may update their own state but must preserve the Compute branch identity and all Compute-owned outputs.
- Do not implement commands, Research per-tick progress, final reveal, Museum behavior, or a Research tick stage.
- Leave every change uncommitted and unstaged.
